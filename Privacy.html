Privacy & Terms

Last updated: 2 November 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how FoundX works.

What we collect

Your name, email and password (handled by Firebase Authentication), and what you choose to add: skills, field, journey, goals, bio, photos, posts, comments, stories and messages. Your email, phone and date of birth, if you give them, are kept in a private record that other members cannot read.

Who can see what

Your profile, posts and stories are visible to other signed-in members. Direct messages are visible only to the people in that chat. Your email and phone are never shown to other members. The site owner can access the database to run and protect the service.

How we use it

To run FoundX: show your profile and posts, let members find each other, send alerts about likes, comments and messages, and keep the community safe. We do not sell your data.

Payments

Paid tags and Premium are paid by UPI. You send your name, email and UPI transaction ID, and we approve the request by hand. Please pay only through the QR code shown inside FoundX. We never ask for your card, PIN or OTP.

Game money

All coins and cash in the games on FoundX are virtual. They have no real-money value.

Community rules

Be respectful. No spam, harassment, hate, scams, fake profiles or illegal content. Do not impersonate others. You can report a post or a person with the 🚩 button, and block someone from their profile. We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Ideas and advice

Ideas, scores and tags on FoundX are not verified and are not financial, legal or investment advice. Do your own checks before you work with someone or send money.

Young members

If you are under 18, please use FoundX with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, and never share your phone number or address in public posts.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and delete your own posts any time. To delete your account and data, message us on WhatsApp at +91 73541 03448 from the same account details and we will remove it.

Cookies and storage

FoundX uses your browser storage to keep you signed in and remember simple settings like dark mode.

Changes

We may update this page as FoundX grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.
